# NEPSE Analyzer — Important Terms

## NEPSE

Nepal Stock Exchange is Nepal’s organized securities market where listed company shares are traded.

## Stock Symbol

A short code used to identify a listed company, such as `NABIL`.

## Current Price

The latest available trading price of a stock.

## Previous Closing Price

The stock’s final recorded price from the previous trading day.

## Point Change

The numerical difference between the current price and previous closing price.


## Percentage Change
Percentage change =
(Point change / Previous closing price) × 100

## Daily Gainer
A stock whose current price is higher than its previous closing price.

## Daily Loser
A stock whose current price is lower than its previous closing price.

## Quarterly Report
A financial report showing a company’s performance during approximately three months.

## Revenue
The company’s total income before expenses, interest and taxes are deducted.

## Net Profit
The money remaining after the company subtracts expenses, interest, taxes and other costs.

## EPS
Earnings per share measures the profit associated with each ordinary share.
EPS = Profit available to ordinary shareholders ÷ Weighted average number of ordinary shares

## Quarter-over-Quarter
QoQ compares the current quarter with the immediately previous quarter.
QoQ change =
((Current quarter − Previous quarter) / Previous quarter) × 100.

## Year-over-Year
YoY compares the current quarter with the same quarter from the previous year.
YoY change =
((Current period − Same period last year) / Same period last year) × 100

## Important Distinction

A positive daily percentage change means the stock price increased, while a negative percentage change means the stock price decreased.

Daily price change measures movement between the previous closing price and current price. Quarterly financial change compares company results, such as revenue, net profit, or EPS, across reporting periods.

These measurements must remain separate because short-term stock-price movement does not necessarily represent a company’s quarterly financial performance.
